What the lecture covers

  1. Measles in Samoa, 2019: a preventable epidemic
  2. Agent, host and environment, and the six links of the chain of infection
  3. Modes of transmission, including WHO's 2024 terms for spread through the air
  4. Attack rate and secondary attack rate: a choir practice in 2020 and Nipah in Kerala in 2018
  5. R0, the formula R0 = β × c × D, and why R0 is not a constant
  6. Effective R, the herd immunity threshold, and when herd immunity fails
  7. Superspreading and the dispersion k
  8. Ring vaccination: smallpox eradication and the 2015 Ebola vaccine trial
  9. Sporadic, endemic, epidemic and pandemic; why epidemics start; epidemic curve shapes
