What the session covers

  1. Coffee and cancer of the pancreas, 1981: a significant, dose-related finding that was probably not real
  2. Four explanations for any association: chance, bias, confounding and cause
  3. Chance: random and systematic error, p-values, confidence intervals, multiple subgroups (ISIS-2 and the star signs), type I and II errors and power
  4. Selection bias: the coffee controls, the healthy worker effect, Berkson's bias, collider bias in COVID-19 studies, losses to follow-up
  5. Information bias: recall, interviewer and observer bias; non-differential and differential misclassification; prevention by design
  6. Confounding: kidney stones, the three conditions, hormone therapy and the Women's Health Initiative, confounding by indication
  7. Controlling confounding: randomisation, restriction, matching, stratification, Mantel-Haenszel and the change-in-estimate rule
  8. Effect modification and interaction: asbestos and smoking, the pill and factor V Leiden, additive and multiplicative scales, RERI
  9. Five questions to ask before believing an association, and viva traps
