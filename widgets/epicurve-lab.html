Epidemic Curve Lab

The shape of an epidemic curve tells you how people were exposed. Pick a pattern and an incubation period, and read the shape.

Pattern

Simulated cases, illustrative. Incubation periods follow a log-normal distribution around the chosen median. In the propagated pattern, the time from one case's infection to the next generation's infection is set equal to the incubation period, and colours alternate between successive generations.

Built for teaching at AIIMS Bhopal. The same shapes appear in real outbreaks; reading them is the first step of every outbreak investigation.
