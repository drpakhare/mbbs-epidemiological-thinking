R is an average. Two infections with the same R can spread very differently. The dispersion k measures how unevenly transmission is shared between cases.

Each case's number of new infections is drawn from a negative binomial distribution with mean R and dispersion k, the standard model since Lloyd-Smith et al. Nature 2005. Large k means everyone transmits about equally; k below 1 means most cases infect nobody and a few infect many. Built for teaching at AIIMS Bhopal.
