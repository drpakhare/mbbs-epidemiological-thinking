Approximate, combining two studies: precancer develops from fewer than 10% of new infections (Schiffman 2007); about 31% of untreated precancers become invasive within 30 years (McCredie 2008).

Timeline: WHO cervical cancer fact sheet, 15 to 20 years from infection to cancer with a normal immune system, 5 to 10 years in women with weakened immunity such as untreated HIV. Precancer typically within 5 to 10 years of a persistent infection (Schiffman M et al. Lancet 2007). Most new infections clear, median duration 8 months (Ho GY et al. N Engl J Med 1998).
