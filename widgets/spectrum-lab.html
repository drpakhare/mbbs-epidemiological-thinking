Iceberg Lab

1,000 people are infected. How many ever fall ill, how many become severely ill, and how many die? Most of the iceberg is under water.

Infection

Pathogenicity = clinical cases / infected. Case fatality = deaths / clinical cases. Infectivity, the share of exposed susceptible people who get infected, needs a count of the exposed, so it is taught separately. Built for teaching at AIIMS Bhopal.
