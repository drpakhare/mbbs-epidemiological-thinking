How much immunity is enough?

Herd immunity threshold = 1 minus 1/R0. Effective R = R0 x share still susceptible. The town is a simulation: each infectious person makes on average R0 infecting contacts, mostly with near neighbours; results vary by chance from run to run. Built for teaching at AIIMS Bhopal.
