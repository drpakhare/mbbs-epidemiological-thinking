What the session covers

  1. Headlines and the lauki story: why one case is not a rate
  2. Clinical questions are epidemiological questions
  3. Snow and cholera: denominators, and the 2 by 2 table
  4. Risk, relative risk, risk difference, attributable fraction and odds ratio
  5. Semmelweis and handwashing: compare, intervene, watch the outcome
  6. Doll and Hill: case-control studies and the odds ratio
  7. Thinking traps: relative vs absolute risk, ecological fallacy, confounding, survivorship bias
  8. The diagnostic 2 by 2: sensitivity, specificity and predictive values
  9. Five questions to ask of any health headline
